## Decisions

- "The manual CSV path" is AC 5's "by hand … exports to CSV". The app has no CSV import, so the e2e
  test makes an idea by hand and reads the export.
- "Settings for RSS and the briefing query time" became two checks. The RSS refresh (15 s per feed,
  feeds in parallel) runs inside the briefing job before its two LLM calls, so all three must fit the
  job expiry. The ready time must leave room for one build attempt (build time + job expiry), or
  `briefing_late` would fire before a build could finish. Neither check changes the defaults:
  15 + 2 × 120 s ≤ 900 s, and 06:30 + 15 min ≤ 07:00.
- The chars/3 token estimate was not changed. It needs the provider's real tokenizer to measure
  against, so it stays open.
- The go/no-go threshold after the probe is Step 4's decision point: success below 90% after tuning
  brings options to the owner. So that open item is resolved, not a question.
- `l.facebook.com` landing domains: no change. On the dev database 34 of 1556 ads have no landing
  domain. Their hosts: `fb.me` 11, `instagram.com` 8, `facebook.com` 2, `www.facebook.com` 2, and 11
  have no link. None of the 4 on a `facebook.com` host is an `l.facebook.com/l.php` link or has a `u=`
  parameter: 2 are Marketplace items and 2 are Facebook pages, which really have no shop domain. Only 1
  has a domain-like `snapshot.caption`, and that field is advertiser text, so it is not used. Unwrapping
  `l.php?u=` would change 0 ads today, so nothing was built and there is nothing to backfill.
- Screenshots were not updated: no screen changed. An e2e run rewrites five pending images. The ideas
  table and board at both sizes show the same ideas in a different order and differ by 0.5–1.5% (see
  "Unverified"). Crawler attention differs by 0.03%, and its cause was not checked.
